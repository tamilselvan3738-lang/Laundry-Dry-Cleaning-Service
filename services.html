The Fabric Sanctuaries

Professional Care
For Every Garment Category

From organic wash cycles and botanical fabric softening to chemical-free dry cleaning and hand-treated leather restorations, our specialized facilities are engineered to extend the life of your wardrobe.

Advanced Fabric Lab

Constant climate vault keeping garments clean & dust-free

Service Code: WF-01

Wash & Fold Care

Our standard daily service covers standard cottons, linens, towels, and activewear. We separate whites, pastels, and darks, using weight-precise biodegradable detergents.

Color Split

We wash lights, darks, and colors strictly in separate drums.

Soft Water

Water is filtered to zero mineral hardness to prevent fabric crusting.

Hygienic Fold

Items are iron-pressed-steam sanitized and packed flat immediately.

Service Code: DC-02

Eco Dry Cleaning

For suits, coats, formal dresses, and delicate weaves. We use non-toxic, bio-degradable solvents that clean down to the fiber core without stripping colors or altering seams.

01

Stain Audit

We inspect each garment under UV scopes to detect oil and soil pigments before wash.

02

Fluid Wash

Garments tumble inside bio-degradable fluid baths that gently dissolve oils.

03

Safe Dry

Drying cycles run under vacuum pressure, evaporating solvent at safe, room temperature.

04

Guard Seal

Each suit is sealed in breathable covers with insect-repelling cedar block notes.

Active Fluid Purge

We extract and purify dry cleaning fluid on-site using dual-stage filters, leaving zero waste chemicals.

Service Code: SP-03

Steam Press & Ironing

Crease-free perfection tailored to fabric structural weight. Our facility employs standard weight compression plates alongside hand-ironing steam stations.

Crisp Collar Pressing

Specialized curved plates align shirt collars and cuffs perfectly, maintaining shape for days.

Delicate Iron Finishes

Handheld heat-monitored irons press pleated skirts and silk blouses individually without burning delicate weave corners.

Service Code: LC-04

Leather & Suede Restoration

Leather and suede shouldn't wash like ordinary cloth. We treat hides with animal-safe hydration oils, manually brush suede pile, and apply pigment restoration coats to dry surfaces.

Natural Oil Rehydration

We massage organic moisturizing balms back into jacket skin to prevent dryness, scaling, and fiber cracks.

Manual Pile Brushing

Suede fibers are brushed by hand using brass-wire bristles, realigning texture map directions.

Service Code: DF-05

Delicate Fabric Care

Silks, lace, wedding gowns, and fine knitted cashmere require soft handling. We employ zero-agitation soaking tubs and flat-shelf air drying racks to eliminate stretching.

  • ✓ Mulberry Silk: Hand-washed in pH-neutral water using botanical extract soaps.
  • ✓ Pure Cashmere: Cleaned using water softeners and lanolin baths to keep wool fluffy.
  • ✓ Beaded & Embroidered Gowns: Cleaned individually by hand inspection, shielding beads from machine drums.
Service Code: PG-06

Premium Garment Care

For designer suits, couture collections, and bespoke garments. Includes dedicated laundry coordinators, tailored finish starch adjustments, and door-to-door concierge delivery options.

The Concierge Pledge

Premium order items are cataloged with high-resolution photography before cleaning. If any thread, button, or weave suffers damage under our care, we replace or repair the item completely.
